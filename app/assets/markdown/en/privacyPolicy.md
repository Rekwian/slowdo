# Privacy Policy

## Data Processing

Slowdo is designed to respect your privacy as much as possible.

The tasks you create are stored locally on your device (IndexedDB). They are not transmitted to the publisher.

At this time, Slowdo does not require you to create a user account.

## Usage Analytics

Slowdo uses **Vercel Analytics** to collect anonymized usage statistics (such as the number of visits, pages viewed, etc.) in order to improve the application.

Vercel Analytics is designed to operate without cookies in its standard configuration.

## Data Sharing

The data stored locally on your device is neither sold nor shared with third parties.

Usage statistics are processed by Vercel in accordance with its Privacy Policy.

## Your Rights

In accordance with applicable regulations, you may contact me with any questions regarding how Slowdo processes data.
